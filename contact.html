Emergency Procedures During Sessions

During Forest School sessions, staff carry:

  • a mobile phone for emergencies
  • a full first aid kit
  • emergency contact information for each child

Parents will be contacted immediately if:

  • their child is injured or unwell
  • a child needs to go home
  • there is a site evacuation or incident

If you need to reach us urgently during a session, please use the contact number provided privately to enrolled families.

Location – Natterjack @ Bath

Be Wild Be Free operates from the beautiful woodland and meadow site known as Natterjack @ Bath. Full directions and meeting points are shared with families and school partners upon enrolment.

We do not share the exact location publicly to protect the privacy of the landowner and the ecological integrity of the space.
