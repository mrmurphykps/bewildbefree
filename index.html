Welcome to Be Wild Be Free

Be Wild Be Free was created to give children the freedom, time and space to explore, discover, and connect with the natural world. We believe that outdoor learning has the power to transform confidence, resilience, emotional wellbeing, physical development and the ability to thrive in a complex, ever-changing world.

Our Forest School sessions are delivered in Bath and the surrounding area, with a strong emphasis on child-led learning, supported risk-taking, and nurturing a deep sense of belonging in nature.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: To connect children with nature through meaningful, joyful and safe outdoor learning experiences.

We create spaces where children feel safe to take risks, challenge themselves, make discoveries, form friendships, and develop practical life skills. Whether building shelters, exploring woodland habitats, climbing trees, learning tool skills or sharing food around a campfire, every moment offers a chance to grow.

Our Ethos

Forest School is more than an outdoor session—it is a long-term, child-centred approach to learning that fosters curiosity, independence, resilience, creativity and emotional intelligence. We honour the six Forest School principles and place children at the heart of everything we do.

  • Child-led learning—children shape the session, explore their own interests and lead their own discoveries.
  • Holistic development—supporting physical, emotional, social, cognitive, spiritual and creative growth.
  • Nature connection—deepening understanding, respect and care for the natural world.
  • Supported risk-taking—understanding how to assess, manage and embrace appropriate risks safely.
  • Community and belonging—fostering collaboration, kindness, empathy and shared responsibility.
  • Skills for life—tool use, fire safety, resilience, problem-solving, communication, emotional literacy and more.

What Is Forest School?

Forest School is a long-term learning process based on regular sessions in natural spaces. It supports learners of all ages to explore, create, experiment and develop a lasting connection to nature.

Originating in Scandinavia and becoming established in the UK in the 1990s, Forest School is now recognised as one of the most powerful ways to nurture confidence, resilience, emotional wellbeing and curiosity in children.

Sessions involve a blend of structured and child-initiated experiences. These might include:

  • den building
  • using tools safely (knives, saws, loppers, drills)
  • learning knots and practical skills
  • fire lighting and campfire cooking
  • tree and plant identification
  • creative nature crafts
  • exploring woodland ecosystems
  • mindfulness and nature connection practices
  • water and mud play

What Makes Be Wild Be Free Unique?

Be Wild Be Free is led by highly experienced outdoor professionals with deep expertise in:

  • Forest School leadership
  • mountain leadership and expedition skills
  • canoeing, climbing and cycling instruction
  • first aid and outdoor emergency practice
  • curriculum-linked outdoor learning

We bring decades of experience in outdoor education, ensuring each session is safe, inspiring and tailored to the needs of the children in our care.

We take health, safety and safeguarding extremely seriously. All activities are carefully risk-assessed, staff are trained and qualified, and children are always encouraged to understand risk and keep themselves and each other safe.
