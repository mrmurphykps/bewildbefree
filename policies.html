Policies and Procedures

This section outlines the key policies and procedures that underpin the safe and effective running of Be Wild Be Free Forest School programmes. These policies ensure compliance with the Forest School Association (FSA) criteria, relevant UK safeguarding laws, and best practice in outdoor learning and risk management.

Safeguarding Policy

Be Wild Be Free is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of all children. We adhere to:

  • Keeping Children Safe in Education (KCSIE)
  • Working Together to Safeguard Children (2023)
  • Local Safeguarding Partnerships guidance

Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL)

  • Lead DSL: Kelly Murphy
  • Deputy DSL: David Murphy

The DSL is responsible for managing concerns, reporting to statutory agencies, and ensuring safeguarding standards.

Safeguarding Principles

  • The welfare of the child is paramount.
  • All children have equal rights to safety and protection.
  • Disclosures are always taken seriously.
  • Information is shared on a need-to-know basis only.
  • Allegations against staff follow statutory procedures.

Responding to a Disclosure

  • Listen carefully—do not interrupt.
  • Reassure the child they have done the right thing.
  • Do not promise confidentiality.
  • Record factual notes immediately.
  • Report to DSL as soon as possible.

All safeguarding concerns are recorded securely and stored in line with UK GDPR.

Health & Safety Policy

Be Wild Be Free ensures the highest standard of safety through robust risk assessment, safe practices, and clear communication. Forest School involves supported risk-taking; risk-benefit assessments are completed for site, tools, fire and activities.

Key Principles

  • Sessions are run by qualified practitioners.
  • Risk assessments are reviewed regularly.
  • Daily site checks are carried out before every session.
  • Tools and equipment are maintained properly.
  • Emergency procedures are clearly communicated.
  • High adult-to-child ratios are maintained (1:6 typical).

First Aid

  • At least one Level 3 Forest School Leader with Outdoor First Aid attends every session.
  • A full forest school first aid kit is carried at all times.
  • Parents are informed of all injuries requiring first aid.

Accident & Incident Reporting

  • All incidents are recorded on an Incident/Accident Form.
  • Forms are shared with parents and stored securely.

Behaviour Policy

Our behaviour expectations are grounded in respect for self, others and nature. We follow the Forest School ethos of supporting emotional development through positive, relational approaches.

Behaviour Expectations

  • Look after yourself.
  • Look after each other.
  • Look after the environment.

Our Approach

  • Positive reinforcement and modelling.
  • Restorative conversations.
  • Emotion coaching language.
  • Teaching boundaries clearly and calmly.
  • Supporting children to resolve conflict safely.

Prohibited Actions

  • No physical punishment or harsh language.
  • No shaming or humiliation.
  • No isolation except for safety.

Persistent unsafe behaviour may require a meeting with parents to assess additional support strategies.

Inclusion Policy

We believe that outdoor learning is for everyone. Our sessions are inclusive, accessible and adaptive to meet a range of needs.

Commitment to Inclusion

  • Reasonable adjustments are made to support all children.
  • Activities are flexible and child-led.
  • SEND needs are welcomed and supported.
  • We consult with parents/carers to understand needs fully.

Children with medical needs are supported through individual risk assessments where required.

Data Protection & Privacy (GDPR)

We collect only the minimum data needed to run safe, effective Forest School programmes. All personal data is stored securely and accessed only by authorised staff.

  • Medical forms and emergency contacts are encrypted or stored securely.
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary.
  • No data is shared without consent except where required for safeguarding or emergency care.

Photography & Media Permission

Photos and videos are only taken with explicit written parental consent. Images are used respectfully and safely, never shared publicly without permission.

Food, Allergies & Eating

Food may be used in certain sessions (e.g., campfire cooking).

  • Allergies are disclosed on medical forms.
  • We follow strict cross-contamination procedures.
  • Children are supervised at all times near fire or hot food.

Toileting Policy

We aim to support children with dignity and privacy. Where no indoor toilet is present, we use environmentally safe toileting procedures.

  • Adults never assist with intimate care unless agreed with parents.
  • Temporary toileting areas follow Leave No Trace principles.

Missing Child Policy

If a child cannot be located, we follow a clear and immediate procedure:

  1. Call “1-2-3 Where Are You?”
  2. Check boundaries and last known areas.
  3. Gather remaining group safely.
  4. Conduct a structured sweep search.
  5. Call emergency services if not located within 10 minutes.
  6. Contact parents once emergency services are alerted.
  7. Record incident fully.

Landowner Agreement & Site Use

Be Wild Be Free operates at Natterjack @ Bath under a landowner permission agreement. This agreement outlines:

  • Approved access days/times
  • Boundaries and permitted areas
  • Environmental protection expectations
  • Fire and tool restrictions (if any)
  • Responsibilities for site care

We take great care to minimise impact, respect wildlife and maintain the land responsibly.

Weather Policy

Sessions continue in all weather except conditions deemed unsafe, including:

  • High winds over 38mph
  • Electrical storms
  • Extreme heat or cold without safe adaptations

Indoor fallback is not available at Natterjack @ Bath. We adapt sessions accordingly.

Staff Code of Conduct

  • Arrive prepared with correct kit and equipment.
  • Model positive behaviour and respect.
  • Complete all risk assessments and checks.
  • Use tools and fire responsibly.
  • Follow safeguarding procedures at all times.
  • Record concerns, incidents or injuries promptly.

Volunteer Policy

Volunteers are welcome and play a valuable role. Before assisting, volunteers must:

  • Read this handbook
  • Sign a volunteer agreement
  • Be briefed on safety procedures
  • Work under the direct supervision of staff

Reviewing Policies

Policies are reviewed annually or following any significant incident, change in guidance, or site alteration.

Updated versions are shared with staff, volunteers and partner settings.
