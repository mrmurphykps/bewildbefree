About Us

Be Wild Be Free is run by experienced outdoor educators Kelly and David Murphy. Together, they bring decades of expertise in Forest School leadership, outdoor adventure, child development, teaching and risk management. Their shared mission is to help children grow through meaningful time outdoors while ensuring the highest standards of safety, professionalism and care.

Our Story

Be Wild Be Free was born from a passion for nature, lifelong outdoor adventure, and a belief that every child deserves the chance to explore, play and grow in the natural world. After years of working across the outdoor industry — from schools and youth organisations to expeditions and national outdoor centres — Kelly and David saw the incredible impact that outdoor learning has on confidence, resilience and wellbeing.

Their dream was to create a Forest School provision that is:

  • professionally designed
  • child-led and inclusive
  • safe, nurturing and inspiring
  • deeply connected to nature

Today, Be Wild Be Free runs Forest School sessions across Bath and beyond, supporting children from early years to primary age and collaborating with schools, families and community groups.

Our Approach to Outdoor Learning

At Be Wild Be Free, learning is:

  • Child-led – play, curiosity and choice are central.
  • Holistic – sessions support emotional, social, physical and creative development.
  • Experiential – learning through doing, exploring and trying again.
  • Inclusive – everyone is welcomed and supported at their level.
  • Risk-aware – we teach children to assess safety and make informed choices.
  • Nature-centred – respecting wildlife, seasons, habitats and sustainability.

We believe that outdoor learning is not an “extra”—it is an essential part of childhood. Our sessions offer opportunities that traditional classrooms cannot: freedom of movement, imagination, fresh air, real risk, and hands-on experiences that build lifelong skills.

Why Outdoor Learning Matters

Research shows that time in nature improves:

  • mental health and emotional regulation
  • communication and social skills
  • physical coordination and strength
  • imagination and problem-solving
  • resilience and perseverance
  • connection and empathy

We see these benefits every day. Children become more confident, more cooperative, more curious, and more grounded in themselves and their environment.
